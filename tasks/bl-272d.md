The published engine (yog 0.0.50) speaks PROTOCOL 17. This crate pins 16
(`src/channel/hello.rs`, mirrored in `src/corpus.rs`). The preface is one
integer compared for equality, so no foot can dial, and since the mismatch
exits rather than retries the unit hits its start limit: `thrall.service` is
`failed` on both live boxes.

This is bl-dc5f / bl-605f / bl-44b4 recurring at the next release train — the
sixth time the pin has gone stale with the suite green. DESIGN 3.6 already
states why nothing on this side of the socket can see it: a fixture cannot see
the far end.

Note that 16 was never a published engine: yog 0.0.49 spoke 15 and 0.0.50
jumped to 17, because 16 (`reply/ops` rows gaining `client`) landed on main and
17 batched two more shapes on top of it inside the same release window.

## The fix, as bl-44b4 did it
Re-vendor at 17 from yog tag v0.0.50: move the two constants, and check every
foot-decoded frame byte for byte against `corpus/*.json` at that tag.

## What moved at 17, and none of it is a foot shape
16 -> 17 is two shapes at one version (yog bl-ebef, bl-6661), both the same
litany 0.0.11 pin landing:
- the signal vocabulary gained the value `truncated` — a new VALUE in
  `signals`, no field moving, which the shape ledger cannot see at all;
- a delivered row on `reply/transcript` and on `reply/inbox` gained an optional
  `sender_name` / `from_name`.

Neither is decoded here. All thirteen frames of the seven shapes a foot speaks
(`request/advertise`, `request/invocations`, `request/complete`,
`reply/advertised`, `reply/invocations`, `reply/routed`, `reply/refusal`) are
byte for byte the text they were at 8; only the equality moves.
